Patchvaneupstream patch tracker

Every patch you sent, from posted to mainline.

Patchvane reads lore.kernel.org, patchwork.kernel.org and git.kernel.org and works out where each of your patches actually is: waiting for a first reply, carrying a Reviewed-by, applied to a maintainer tree, sitting in linux-next, or merged.

  • Nothing is written to any kernel list. It only reads.
  • Sign in and it tracks your address. Nobody sees anybody else's.
  • Reviewer addresses are masked before they leave the machine.
